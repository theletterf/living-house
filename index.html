Training mode

      
This app adapts to you

      
While you use it, it watches what you click, where you pause and what you select, and adjusts itself: wording, layout and styles can change to make things easier to find.

      
You can also ask for changes: type in the bar above the app, or right-click anything and choose what should change.

      
It picks up from the layout you kept last time.

      
Before each change it suggests on its own, a small note will ask you first. Choose Apply to make the change or Not now to skip it.

      
Nothing leaves your browser. Changes can be undone at any time, and the Help always matches what you see.
